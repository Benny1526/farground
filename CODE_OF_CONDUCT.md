# Code of conduct

Farground's issues, discussions and pull requests are for everyone who plays with it or wants to
improve it, whatever their experience, background or language.

## Expected
- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to modding or to GitHub.
- Assume good intent, and keep criticism about the code or the idea, not the person.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the mod, and explain disagreements calmly.

## Not accepted
- Insults, harassment, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or repeatedly derailing a conversation.
- Anything that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome or unsafe.

## What happens
Comments that break these rules may be edited or deleted, and people who keep breaking them may be
blocked from the repository.

To report a problem privately, message BennyPlayz on
[Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/farground) or
[CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1767570). Reports are kept confidential.

This code of conduct is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
